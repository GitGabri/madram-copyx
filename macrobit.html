A review of MacroBit

The advertised day does not fit the spreads.

MacroBit asks you to lock a deposit and be paid 0.6% to 1.8% every day. The price gaps it publishes are a fraction of that.

Opening

MacroBit, at macrobit.ai, sells crypto arbitrage. The product is called QuQi AI. In the automated plans you deposit, the principal stays locked for 90 or 150 days, and the site says profit is credited about every four hours. A manual mode lets you approve individual signals. There is also a referral programme that pays a share of downline profit across many levels.

This page is a review of what the site publishes, read the same way I read an account: a starting figure, a later figure, and whether the second one can come from the first. It is not a record of my own MacroBit balance. I have no deposit there to report.

0.6–1.8%
The daily band on automated plans. Starter sits at the low end, Corporate at the high end. Principal is locked until the term ends.
0.19%
The average spread MacroBit shows on its own public feed. Most rows are smaller. Fees on both legs of a trade come out of that gap.
20 levels
Referral payouts, a large share of downline profit, and further levels tied to team turnover. Arbitrage spreads do not fund a structure like that.

How the published results line up

Three figures from MacroBit’s own materials. The middle one is the only one that looks like a market. The other two are promises.

  1. The plan

    A fixed daily credit

    Starter runs roughly $100 to $10,000 at 0.6–1.0% a day. Professional and Corporate step the band up, toward 1.4–1.8% on the largest deposits. At the low end, uncompounded, that is more than double your money in a year. At the high end it is several times that. Real cross-exchange gaps on liquid coins are basis points, not a percent a day, every day, for a locked term.

  2. The feed

    An average spread of 0.19%

    The site’s public activity shows an average spread around 0.19%, with many rows nearer 0.10–0.17%. After taker fees and slippage, a gap that small is close to a wash. It cannot be the source of a guaranteed 0.6% credit every day.

  3. The account

    A balance on their ledger

    Deposits are crypto only. The site describes funds held in its own wallets, and trades filled on the platform’s liquidity. You are not holding coins at Binance or OKX. Withdrawals are advertised with a 1% fee. If the daily credit is not coming from spreads, it is coming from later deposits.

What the review finds

The site is polished. The economics are not. I would not fund it.

  1. 01

    The product is a yield plan

    Automated mode is a locked deposit with a published daily band, not a tool that leaves your coins on an exchange you already use. Manual mode sells a quota of signals. Neither one is the copy-trading account on the other page.

  2. 02

    The two numbers disagree

    0.6% a day and a 0.19% gross spread cannot both be the result of the same trades. The spread is the market. The daily band is the advertisement.

  3. 03

    The referral layer is the tell

    Paying many levels a large cut of “profit,” and in places a cut of invested principal, is how a deposit scheme recruits. It is not how an arbitrage desk pays for inventory. Public write-ups through November 2026 rate the site high risk, and the Malta company claim does not show up in a registry check those reviews could verify.

How this sits next to the other page

One page is an account I can point at. This one is a set of claims that fail their own arithmetic.

The copy-trading record is three dates and a balance that stayed in a tool after I had already taken the stake home. MacroBit’s public results are a daily percentage, a spread feed, and a referral table. Lined up the same way, they do not tell a story of a working book. They tell a story of a credit that is larger than the gap it claims to harvest.

If someone near you is looking at MacroBit because the site looks finished, the useful comparison is the one above: the promised day, the published spread, and where the money actually sits. A finished website is not a completed trade.

Figures here are taken from MacroBit’s public pages and from independent reviews dated through November 2026. They are not my deposits, and they are not a forecast.

This is a personal review of public claims. It is not advice, and it is not an offer. Crypto products that promise a fixed daily return can lose the entire deposit. Referral income is not evidence that a strategy works. If you are considering any platform, read who holds the funds and whether the stated returns can come from the stated trades.
