The tools

Opening

On May 12, 2026 I deposited about $15,000 into a copy-trading tool I had taken the time to understand. It places the trades. I do not. On September 4 I withdrew about $17,000. That was my $15,000 back, plus $2,000 in profit. The money I had at risk came home.

What stayed in the account was no longer money I needed to recover. As of November 20, a few dollars under $17,000 is still invested, and it is still growing. All of that is a bonus. When I feel like it, I hit reinvest, so those gains compound. There is no chart I have to master. The tool does the work.

Why I am sharing it
