A personal record

Twenty quiet months, and a real result.

I funded one account, left it to follow a single strategy, and stayed out of its way.

Opening

In March 2025 I put $40,000 into copy trading. I have not added to it, and I have not taken anything out. At the close of October 2026 the account stood at $59,392. The gain is $19,392. It did not arrive in a rush. Most months finished a little ahead, a few gave something back, and the stretch as a whole is clearly in my favor.

I am writing it down because the result is worth sharing, and because the way it happened is easier to trust than the way copy trading is usually described. There was no moment I had to catch. There was a decision at the start, and then a year and a half of leaving it alone.

What I actually do

Very little, on purpose. The result has come from three habits, not from watching the market.

  1. 01

    One strategy

    I watched before I funded anything. I wanted a record long enough to include bad weeks, and a style that did not depend on one extraordinary trade. I still follow that same strategy. I stopped looking for a better one once this one had earned the patience.

  2. 02

    Money I can leave

    Forty thousand was large enough that the result would matter to me, and small enough that a losing month would not force my hand. That boundary has done more than any adjustment I might have made along the way.

  3. 03

    A light touch

    I look in on Sundays. I read the month when it closes. I do not step in because a week felt exciting, or because it felt dull. Copy trading has worked for me only because I let the copying be the work.

Why I am sharing it

The ordinary months are the ones I trust.

Copy trading, said simply, is this. A trader you choose places the trades, and your account mirrors them in proportion to what you put in. There is no chart I need to master before breakfast. There is a decision at the start, and then the discipline to leave it.

People near me asked whether this was real, and whether they could do something similar. The honest answer is the page you are reading. It has been running, in my name, for twenty months, and it is ahead by a little under twenty thousand dollars. The loud version of copy trading is not the one I have been living with. This version is slower. It has also been profitable.

If you have savings you can genuinely leave untouched, and you can sit through a month like October 2025 without abandoning the plan, then it is worth a serious look. Not a shortcut. A serious one. Take your time over the strategy, read a full stretch of its losing weeks, and only then decide what to fund.

I wrote this so I could hand someone one page, instead of telling the story differently every time.

This is a personal record. It is not advice, and it is not an offer to manage anyone else’s money. Copy trading can lose money, including the sum you start with. Four of these twenty months lost money. Past performance is not a guide to what happens next. If you try something similar, read the risks of the platform you choose, and use money you do not need for anything else.
